## Coverage change

Every worldspace present in the previous candidate has identical per-tier
chunk counts. The increase from 1,693 to 4,673 chunks comes entirely from two
worldspaces the previous candidate skipped for unresolved LAND diffuse inputs:

worldspace|tier 4|tier 8|tier 16
DLC2SolstheimWorld|2,236|576|144

Solstheim's worldspace defines 34,558 exterior cells (grid -64..127 on both
axes), mostly open ocean, so it carries more LOD than Tamriel (11,187 cells).
The compiler reports 39 worldspace warnings (40 before).

The plugin export warns that `Skyrim.esm` (one GMST) and `Dawnguard.esm` (one
ACTI) each name a master index past their master list; #89 keeps both as the
plugin's own records.
